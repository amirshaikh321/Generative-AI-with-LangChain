Royal Enfield Continental GT 650

The Royal Enfield Continental GT 650 is a modern classic café racer motorcycle
manufactured by Royal Enfield. It is known for its retro styling combined with
modern engineering and performance.

The GT 650 is powered by a 648cc, air-oil cooled, parallel-twin engine.
This engine produces approximately 47 horsepower and 52 Nm of torque.
It is paired with a 6-speed gearbox and a slipper clutch, which provides
smooth gear shifts and better control during aggressive downshifting.

One of the key highlights of the Continental GT 650 is its café racer design.
It features clip-on handlebars, rear-set foot pegs, a sculpted fuel tank,
and a sporty riding posture that appeals to motorcycle enthusiasts.
The motorcycle is built on a steel tubular double cradle frame developed
in collaboration with Harris Performance.

The GT 650 comes equipped with dual-channel ABS, disc brakes on both front
and rear wheels, and twin rear shock absorbers.
The suspension setup offers a balanced ride suitable for both city riding
and highway cruising.

Overall, the Royal Enfield Continental GT 650 is praised for its strong engine
performance, classic aesthetics, refined build quality, and excellent value
for money. It is a popular choice among riders who enjoy retro-styled
motorcycles with modern reliability.
